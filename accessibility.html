Inclusive learning

Accessibility statement

The interface aims to be usable with a keyboard, screen reader and a range of device sizes. Some functionality depends on browser support and is still a work in progress.

Current measures

  • Semantic HTML landmarks and a skip link to the main content.
  • Visible keyboard focus indicators and buttons with descriptive names.
  • Form fields have associated labels, and important status messages use live regions.
  • Content is available as regular text; core reading pages do not require JavaScript to reveal their main text.
  • Layouts adapt to small screens, and reduced-motion preferences are respected.
  • Color is not the sole way used to communicate quiz correctness; the feedback also includes text.

Known limitations

  • Speech recognition and speech synthesis vary by browser, device, installed voices and permissions.
  • Translation and weather features need an internet connection and may be temporarily unavailable.
  • The vocabulary quiz and flashcards have not been independently certified against a formal accessibility standard.

Report a barrier

Use the contact page to report an issue. Include the page, device, browser, assistive technology if applicable, and the action you were trying to complete. Please do not include confidential information.
