Everyday Norwegian phrases and English meanings

Use these short phrases as building blocks for everyday conversations in Norway. Read the Norwegian sentence first, check the English meaning, then use the audio button if your browser has a Norwegian speech voice installed.

Pronunciation tip: browser speech is a convenience, not a perfect native-speaker recording. Voices and accent quality differ between devices. For important conversations, use a trusted human speaker or teacher for feedback.

These examples use Norwegian Bokmål. Spoken Norwegian varies by region, and some expressions have more than one natural translation depending on context.

How to practise a phrase

  1. Read the Norwegian phrase aloud before listening.
  2. Listen once and repeat it slowly, then repeat it at a natural pace.
  3. Replace one word to make the phrase personal, such as changing the food or place.
  4. Review the same phrase a day later and try to recall it without reading.

Questions about Norwegian phrases

Are these phrases Bokmål or Nynorsk?

The phrases on this page are written in Bokmål. Spoken Norwegian differs by region, and Nynorsk is the other official written standard.

Can I trust browser pronunciation?

It is useful for a first approximation, but browser voice support and language quality vary. Use native-speaker recordings and conversations to build listening skills.

How many phrases should I learn at once?

Start with five to eight expressions you can use immediately. Recall them in short sentences and revisit them after a break instead of trying to memorise the whole page in one session.
