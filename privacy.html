Privacy information

Privacy policy

This page explains what the current browser-based learning prototype stores locally and which features connect to external services.

Practice and learning progress

Recent sentence translations and vocabulary/quiz progress are saved in your browser's local storage on the device you use. The current version does not offer user accounts or cloud synchronisation. Anyone with access to your browser profile may be able to view or clear local browser data. Use the site's clear/reset controls or browser settings to remove the saved information.

Translation requests

When you submit a sentence to the translator, the text is sent from your browser to the external translation endpoint currently configured in the prototype. Do not submit passwords, identity information, health information, confidential work material or other sensitive content. The endpoint is a prototype integration, not a service with a privacy agreement controlled by this project.

Speech recognition and pronunciation

Voice input uses the speech-recognition capability provided by your browser and may involve processing by your browser/service provider. Speech output uses the available device voices. The exact handling of voice data depends on that platform, so check its privacy documentation before using voice features.

Weather information

The home page requests modelled current weather for Oslo and Bergen from Open-Meteo. The request includes the selected public coordinates, not your device location.

Offline caching

If service workers are supported and enabled, the site may cache selected pages and static files to make learning content available offline after an initial online visit. Translation and weather still need an internet connection. Browser settings can be used to clear cached site data.

Contact

For questions or feedback, use the developer contact page. Please avoid sending sensitive information by email.

Changes

This notice describes the prototype as currently implemented and may need to change if the site adds accounts, a backend or different third-party services.
