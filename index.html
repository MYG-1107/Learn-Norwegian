Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the learning tool.

What is Norwegian Bokmål?
Bokmål is one of the two official written standards of Norwegian. This site focuses on everyday practice in Bokmål.
Do I need a microphone?
Only for voice input. You can always use the text field instead.
Where is my recent practice stored?
Recent translations are stored in your browser's local storage on the device you use. They are not presented as an account-based history.
Is this an official NAV or UDI service?
No. Learn Norwegian is an independent learning project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by NAV or UDI.
