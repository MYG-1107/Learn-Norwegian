Common questions

Learning Norwegian: FAQs

Is this Norwegian learning website free?

Yes. The lessons, phrasebook, vocabulary cards and quiz on this site are free to use. The translation and live weather features depend on external services and an internet connection.

Should a beginner learn Bokmål or Nynorsk?

This website focuses on Bokmål, one of Norway's two official written standards. It is a practical starting point for everyday written Norwegian, but you may also encounter Nynorsk and regional spoken dialects.

Can I learn Norwegian without a microphone?

Yes. You can type text, study the beginner guide, browse the phrasebook, use flashcards and take quizzes without a microphone. Pronunciation playback depends on available browser voices.

Does the website save my progress?

The vocabulary and quiz progress is stored in your browser on the device you use. There is no account-based cloud sync in this version. Clearing browser storage removes local progress.

Will this website make me fluent?

No single website can guarantee fluency. Use these tools for short daily practice alongside listening, reading, conversations, feedback and a structured course matched to your goals.
